# Brand and Messaging

## Identity

- Company: January Labs
- Product: Limerence
- Primary color: coral `#ff4d00`
- Aesthetic: Brutalist Terminal Editorial
- Tagline: **Turn databases into conversational data agents**

## Approved messages

- Stop being the bottleneck.
- Ask your database like you would ask a colleague.
- Get analyst-quality insights grounded in real data.
- A complete data-agent workspace on your desktop.
- Your LLM keys, your costs, your control.
- Five minutes to value, not five months.

## Message hierarchy

1. Lead with the user's operational pain and desired outcome.
2. Explain conversational, business-aware access to real company data.
3. Establish trust through the local desktop runtime, read-only access,
   customer-owned keys, and zero telemetry.
4. Support the promise with domain teaching, insights, dashboards, reports,
   and automations.
5. Keep claims consistent with pre-launch beta status; never invent proof.
