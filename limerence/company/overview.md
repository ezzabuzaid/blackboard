# Company and Product Overview

## Identity

- Company: January Labs
- Product: Limerence
- Website: https://limerence.sh/
- Tagline: Turn databases into conversational data agents
- Stage: pre-launch beta; no paying customers yet

## What Limerence is

Limerence Desktop is a local application for working with database agents,
conversations, dashboards, reports, automations, and data sources in one
workspace. People ask business questions in plain language and receive
analyst-quality insights grounded in real data without writing SQL or waiting
for an operations person to pull a report.

## Problem

Organizations hold valuable operational data in databases and spreadsheets,
but most people cannot query it directly. Data owners become a bottleneck,
teams wait for ad-hoc exports, and recurring requests displace analysis and
strategic work.

## Solution

Limerence creates agents that:

- understand business terminology, rules, and organizational context;
- return insights, charts, KPI cards, and data tables rather than raw SQL;
- work through a native desktop workspace with chat, dashboards, reports, and
  scheduled automations;
- improve through structured business knowledge and user corrections;
- connect to local or network-accessible data using user-owned model-provider
  accounts.

## Differentiators

- Desktop-native, with the product runtime contained in the application
- No customer-data collection or product telemetry
- Customer-owned LLM keys with direct provider calls and no markup
- Read-only database connections by default
- Structured domain knowledge and business-rule teaching
- Dashboards, reports, and scheduled automations in the same workspace
