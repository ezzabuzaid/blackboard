# Vision and Direction

## Vision

Make working with business databases approachable through one reliable desktop
workspace for agents, conversations, dashboards, reports, and automations.

## Current stage

Limerence is a pre-launch beta. Core product capabilities are implemented, but
the business has no paying customers yet. Recommendations must optimize for
first meaningful user value, validation, and learning rather than pretending
the company already has scale.

## Near-term direction

- Stronger structured and scheduled reporting
- Reusable report templates
- Scheduling frequently used shortcuts
- Better first-run provider and data-source setup
- Reliable desktop installation, recovery, and updates

## Longer-term direction

- Trend, anomaly, forecast, and comparative analysis
- Data-powered agent workflows with approved actions
- Simpler desktop installation, backup, recovery, and updates

## Explicit exclusions

- No ETL or data-transformation platform
- No real-time streaming-data platform
- No vertical-specific product strategy
- No complexity without demonstrated user value
